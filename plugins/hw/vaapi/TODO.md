# pf-vaapi, open items

## VP9 and AV1 encode, `vaapivp9enc` and `vaapiav1enc`

Both sit on one element shell (`src/encshell.rs`, a `Codec` gives the profile, the
alignment and the per-picture parameter buffers). Both are written after ffmpeg's
`vp9_vaapi` and `av1_vaapi`. They are registered only where the driver lists the encode
entrypoint. Built directly elsewhere, they say so once and drop the frames
(`tests/new_encoders.rs`).

- [ ] Run both on hardware that encodes them, Arc, Meteor Lake or AMD. Neither has run on
  a driver with the entrypoint, the Intel iHD machine they were written on has no VP9 or
  AV1 encode. Expect fixes on the first run.
- [ ] AV1: carry real loop filter and CDEF values. The frame header sends loop filter and
  CDEF off at a fixed quantizer, as ffmpeg's does, and under a bitrate target CDEF is
  enabled with zero strengths for the driver to rewrite.
- [ ] Give the shell the props and live rate control the H.264, HEVC and VP8 encoders
  have (`qp`, `bitrate` live, `gop`, `hrd`, `force-keyframe`), and a `bytes` escape offer.

The AV1 sequence and frame headers are checked against dav1d (`tests/av1_headers.rs`,
through libdav1d-sys, the parser pf-av1 uses). The libva AV1 parameter layouts come from
the C compiler, `gen/av1_layout.c` into `src/av1enc/layout.rs`, regenerated by
`cargo xtask layout vaapi-av1` in the devshell. Where dav1d keeps what it parsed comes from
`gen/dav1d_layout.c` into `tests/common/dav1d_layout.rs`, regenerated by
`cargo xtask layout vaapi-dav1d`.

## VP8 on the shell

The `Codec` side fits VP8: as VP9, the driver writes the whole frame, no packed headers,
a sequence buffer on a key frame and a picture and a quantizer matrix buffer every frame,
one 8-bit profile, 16 pixel macroblocks. What does not fit is what `vaapivp8enc` has
around its buffers and the shell does not: the props and their live retargets (a bitrate
or HRD change resends the misc buffers mid-GOP, `RcUpdate`), the HRD misc buffer, the
`bytes` escape, and VP8's 0 to 127 quantizer where `Settings::qindex` is VP9's and AV1's
0 to 255. Once the shell has props and live rate control (above) VP8 moves onto it with
only its parameter buffers left in `vp8enc.rs`. Not done.
